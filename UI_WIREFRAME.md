## Color Palette & Typography

### Colors:
- **Primary:** #4F46E5 (Indigo Blue)
- **Secondary:** #8B5CF6 (Purple)
- **Accent:** #10B981 (Green) - for success, approve
- **Danger:** #EF4444 (Red) - for reject, cancel
- **Warning:** #F59E0B (Amber) - for urgent
- **Background:** #F8FAFC (Light Gray)
- **Card Background:** #FFFFFF (White)
- **Text Dark:** #0F172A (Dark Slate)
- **Text Light:** #64748B (Muted Gray)
- **Border:** #E2E8F0 (Light Border)

### Typography:
- **Headings:** Poppins Bold, 24px-32px
- **Subheadings:** Poppins SemiBold, 18px-20px
- **Body Text:** Inter Regular, 14px-16px
- **Labels:** Inter SemiBold, 12px-14px
- **Buttons:** Inter Bold, 14px-16px

---

## Component Design Guidelines

### Buttons:
- **Primary Button:** Blue bg, white text, 12px padding, rounded corners
- **Secondary Button:** Gray bg, dark text
- **Danger Button:** Red bg, white text
- **Success Button:** Green bg, white text

### Cards:
- White background, 2px subtle shadow, 12px border radius
- Padding: 16px
- Hover effect: slight shadow increase

### Form Inputs:
- 1px border, light gray
- Padding: 10px 12px
- Border radius: 8px
- Focus state: blue border, light blue bg

### Status Badges:
- Open: Green
- In Progress: Blue
- Submitted: Amber
- Completed: Green
- Rejected: Red

---

## Animation & Interactions:
- Smooth transitions: 0.3s ease
- Hover effects on cards and buttons
- Loading spinners for async operations
- Fade-in animations on page load
- Slide-up modals
